## 3. Lighting & Mood Arc

Every video uses a deliberate **3-Act Lighting Progression** to amplify the comedy:
1. **Act 1 — Crisp Daylight (`daylight`)**: Bright blue sky, clean hard sunlight, energetic setup.
2. **Act 2 — Warm Golden-Hour Honeymoon (`sunset_golden`)**: Saturated orange-peach sunset sky with warm golden rim light and sparkling stars while the experiment feels like paradise.
3. **Act 3 — Dark Rainstorm Disaster (`storm_rain`)**: Moody slate-blue storm sky, cooler directional light, diagonal pouring rain overlay, and warm indoor desk-lamp contrast (`indoor_cozy`) for comedic chaos.

## 4. Signature Graphic Comedy Overlays & Transitions

- **Impact White Flash Cut**: Crisp 4–6 frame white flash transition on the hook twist (*"but it went horribly wrong."*).
- **Full-Screen Wall Calendar Page Tear**: Illustrated spiral wall calendar (`WEEK 1` filled with sunny hearts/smiles → animated page peeling/flying off → `WEEK 4` filled with gloomy rain clouds and a frown).
- **Rubber Stamp Slams**: Bold green `"APPROVED ✓"` badge and tilted crimson `"BANNED"` / `"NO RULES!"` / `"RETIRED"` rubber-stamp overlays slamming onto screen with spring overshoot and subtle impact shake.
- **Escalation Gauges & Gadget Gags**: Animated rising red mercury thermometer/meter (`dB` noise level or `$` bread price), sparking electric zaps, and a glowing router flashing a bold red slashed **NO WI-FI** icon.

## 5. Story Structure & Pacing (~50–55 Seconds)

1. **Explosive "What If?" Hook (`0–6s`)**: Crowded city crosswalk visual gag + protagonist reveal + *"but it went horribly wrong"* white flash.
2. **The Experiment & Rules (`6–16s`)**: Aerial reveal + entrance signboard + `"APPROVED ✓"` rush of excited citizens.
3. **Golden-Hour Honeymoon (`16–24s`)**: Warm sunset celebration montage showing why the idea looked amazing at first.
4. **Calendar Flip Pivot (`24–27s`)**: `WEEK 1 → WEEK 4` page rip transitions instantaneously into stormy reality.
5. **Rule-of-Three Escalating Failures (`27–44s`)**:
   - Failure 1: Indoor/personal comfort breaks down (rising red gauge/meter).
   - Failure 2: Community infrastructure fails in pouring rain.
   - Failure 3 (*"The final straw"*): Sparking router kills Wi-Fi + dramatic red rubber stamp slam punchline.
6. **Ironic Exodus & Punchy Takeaway (`44–53s`)**: Citizens pack suitcases / reverse course one by one with a memorable, witty closing line.
