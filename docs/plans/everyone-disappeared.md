# Next Short: "What If Everyone on Earth Disappeared?" (~40 s)

Same storytime style and cast as Freeze Time: Alex narrates in the first person, with calm ~150 wpm voice, 1-word captions, viseme lip-sync and the warm toon look. The topic comes from the YouTube autocomplete demand list ("everyone disappeared", "last man on earth").

## Idea in one line
Alex wakes up and everyone on Earth is gone. He has a fun day (free pizza, he becomes mayor, he dances in the street), then a lonely night, until he hears noises behind Granny's door. Everyone was hiding to throw him a surprise birthday party.

- **Hook (0–3 s):** a wide shot of an empty street, Alex tiny in the middle, with "I woke up... and everyone on Earth was gone."
- **Stake counter:** PEOPLE LEFT: 1 throughout, then it jumps 1 → 5 on "SURPRISE!".
- **Twist:** the disappearance was a surprise party. It's a warm, rewatchable payoff, and the CTA asks "Would YOU have found them sooner?"
- **Continuity gags from Freeze Time:** the pigeon's empty perch (even the pigeon is gone), then the pigeon pops out with everyone at the party.

## Visual upgrades (not overdone)
- Light depth of field on close-ups, so the background softens and the speaker pops.
- Time-of-day lighting: morning (warm) → noon (bright) → night (blue + warm lamps) → party (warm + colored bulbs).
- More camera variety: very wide shots to sell the emptiness, a low hero angle for "mayor", a close-up for the twist.
- Ambient life in every shot: swaying trees, drifting clouds, something always moving, so no frame feels frozen (unlike the Freeze Time look).
